This is the readme file for Okazaki-w010_Trigger.csv
# Hazards
 - w010-t34_rainfall
   - Basin-averaged accumulated rainfall (mm) for the Yahagi River at Level 2 (L2).

 - w010-t16_rainfall
   - Basin-averaged accumulated rainfall (mm) for the Yahagi River at Level 1 (L1).

 - w010-t34_Iwatsu
   - Water depth (m) at the Iwatsu Water Observation Station from HQ data at Level 2 (L2).
 - w010-t16_Iwatsu
   - Water depth (m) at the Iwatsu Water Observation Station from HQ data at Level 1 (L1).

# Evacuation Scenario Descriptions
 - L2-L1_evacuation_order
   - Scenario where Level 2 (L2) is predicted and evacuation orders are issued accordingly, but Level 1 (L1) actually occurs.

 - L1-L2_evacuation_order
   - Scenario where Level 1 (L1) is predicted and evacuation orders are issued accordingly, but Level 2 (L2) actually occurs.

 - L2-L1-B_evacuation_order
   - Same as L2-L1_evacuation_order, with an additional evacuation order issued for residents near the Otogawa River.

 - L1-L2-B_evacuation_order
   - Same as L1-L2_evacuation_order, with an additional evacuation order issued for residents near the Otogawa River.
