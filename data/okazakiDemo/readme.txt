RoadCensus.shp

Data source:
1. Japan 2021 Road Census data:
 https://www.mlit.go.jp/road/ir/ir-data/census_visualizationR3/index.html
2. Road network shp data handwritten by NITech team

Data attribution:
ID: Road Census data's 11-digits "Traffic Survey Basic Section Number(交通調査基本区間番号)"
	https://www.mlit.go.jp/road/ir/ir-data/census_visualizationR3/index.html
City Code Pref: A code representing the specific city and prefecture where the data was collected.
Section No: A unique identifier for each road section or unit area in the survey.
Road Type: The classification of the road (e.g., highway, local road, expressway).
Route No: The official route number assigned to the road.
Observation Type: The category of observation for the traffic survey, indicating the purpose or scope (e.g., regular, special observation).(unsure; need clarification)
Data Usage: Whether it includes individual survey (unsure; need clarification)
Observation 12_24hr: Indicates whether the traffic data was observed over a 12-hour or a 24-hour period.
Observation Date: The specific date on which the traffic data was collected.
Weather: The recorded weather conditions on the observation day (e.g., sunny, rainy).
Direction: Indicates the traffic flow direction for the data (e.g., inbound, outbound).
Vehicle Type: The classification of vehicles included in the observation (e.g., cars, trucks, motorcycles).
7 AM to 6 AM: Hourly traffic volume data, indicating the number of vehicles passing through the section during each hour from 7 AM on one day to 6 AM on the next day.
12hr Traffic: The total traffic volume counted over a 12-hour period, typically during daytime hours.
24hr Traffic: The total traffic volume counted over a full 24-hour period.
